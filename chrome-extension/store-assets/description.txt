SHORT SUMMARY (132-char field)
Auto-fill any Google Form with your verified Google identity through Accord — name, email, and your saved answers, in one tap.


DETAILED DESCRIPTION

Stop retyping the same answers into every Google Form.

Accord adds a small "Auto-fill with Accord" button to the corner of any Google Form. Tap it, and the form reopens with your details already in place — name, email, college, and anything else you've saved to your Accord profile — with each filled question softly highlighted so you can see exactly what changed before you submit.

Works where other auto-fillers can't. Because the button reads the form from your own signed-in browser, it handles forms Accord's servers can't open on their own — including forms restricted to an organisation that only a member can view. The first time anyone opens such a form through Accord, its questions are remembered, so the next person to open it — on any device, with or without the extension — gets auto-fill too.

Set up once, done forever. Save your details at accord-ingly.netlify.app, then every form you open just fills itself.

Private by design.
- No account to create in the extension.
- No analytics, no tracking, no background data collection.
- Runs only on Google Forms pages — no access to your other tabs or browsing.
- Reads a form's public question structure (not your answers) to build your prefill link.

Accord is open source: github.com/modkavartini/accord
