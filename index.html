PRIVACY POLICY

What Accord stores about you

Accord keeps only what it needs to recognise you and fill the fields you set up. Your form answers go straight to Google.

What we collect

  • Google account identity — name, email and avatar, as Google returns them.
  • Profile rules — the fields and values you save.
  • Accords you create — form URL, alias and field IDs.

What we don't collect

  • Your form answers — they go from your browser straight to Google.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or ads.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.

Third parties

Sign-in is Google Sign-In; data lives in Google Cloud Firestore — both under Google's own terms.

Deleting your data

Delete an Accord or a profile field from your dashboard and it's gone immediately. To wipe everything, open the user menu on the dashboard and choose Delete account.
