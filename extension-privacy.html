extension privacy

Accord for Google Forms · updated 23 Sep 2026

Accord for Google Forms is a Chrome extension that adds an “Auto-fill with Accord” button to Google Forms. This page explains exactly what it touches. The short version: it holds no account, stores nothing about you, and adds no tracking.

No data is collected by the extension. It has no analytics, no remote logging, and no background data collection. It requests no browser permissions beyond running on docs.google.com/forms pages.

what it accesses

  • On a Google Form page, it reads the form's public question definition (question labels, types and choice options) — the same data the form already sends to your browser to render itself.
  • It reads the page URL to tell a form from the Accord return link, and to highlight the questions Accord prefilled.

It does not read your answers, your Google account credentials, other tabs, your browsing history, or any page that isn't a Google Form.

what leaves your browser

When you click “Auto-fill with Accord”, the form's question definition is passed to the Accord web app (accord.modka.is-a.dev) so it can build your prefilled link. It travels in the new tab's URL fragment (the part after #), which browsers never send over the network — the Accord page reads it locally.

Your name, email and saved answers live in your Accord profile and are applied by the Accord web app, not by the extension. How Accord handles that is covered by the main Accord site. The extension itself sends nothing to any server.

form caching

So the next person to open the same form doesn't wait, Accord may cache a form's question structure (never anyone's answers), keyed by the form's ID. This happens on Accord's side, not in the extension.

permissions

The extension runs a content script only on https://docs.google.com/forms/*. It declares no tabs, storage, cookies, history or “all sites” permissions.

contact

Questions or issues: open an issue on GitHub. The full source of the extension is in that repository under chrome-extension/.
